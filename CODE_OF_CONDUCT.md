# Community conduct

FoodShare welcomes contributors and participating community organizations regardless of identity, experience or resources. Treat donors, receivers and others with dignity. Critique technical decisions using evidence and allow people to correct mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, unwanted contact and publishing identifying beneficiary information are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project. Obtain explicit permission before sharing a partner's story or photograph.

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer. If a private channel is unavailable, request one without identifying the affected person publicly. A maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves and seek an independent reviewer where possible. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or suspend access according to severity. Reporters may request reconsideration by an uninvolved reviewer. Share case details only with people needed to resolve the report.
