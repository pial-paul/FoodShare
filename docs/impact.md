# Impact measurement and pilot protocol

## What the software can measure

Every offer has a donor and date. Each request belongs to one receiver. Only an assigned receiver can confirm an approved handoff. Dated, completed requests are the reporting unit. They are self-reported receipts. Keep demo, test, staging and pilot databases separate.

Run `php bin/impact-report.php YYYY-MM` for a UTC calendar month. The JSON contains confirmed pickups, pickups with measured weight, summed reported kg and distinct receiving accounts. A null weight sum means no recorded weights. Report weight coverage as weighed pickups / confirmed pickups and show both counts. Leave coverage undefined for an empty period. One account may represent an entire shelter or multiple collectors. There is no person count in this schema.

## Field protocol

1. **Recruit and agree scope.** Invite one kitchen and one receiving organization serving the same area. These are proposed participants. Define accepted foods, handling responsibility, operating hours, escalation contact, consent, retention and who can pause the pilot. Use a coordinator experienced in local redistribution.
2. **Establish a comparison.** Proposed starting design is two comparable weeks of baseline logs and four weeks of supervised use. Extend if volumes are low or seasonal patterns differ. Record both weeks' operating days, existing donations, surplus offered, disposal destination, staff minutes and collection limitations. Predefine success thresholds together before launch.
3. **Capture outcomes at handoff.** At receipt, weigh edible contents using a consistent tare method if scales and staff time allow. Skip the weight otherwise. Keep an aggregate exception log for partial loads, uncollected offers, late confirmations and rejected items. Record reasons without recipient names or medical details. The current app treats a listing as a whole pickup. Record partial quantities separately and reconcile them before interpreting the total.
4. **Reconcile weekly.** Compare receipt IDs to the receiver's independent collection log using a sample agreed in advance. Keep an operator correction register with record ID, reason, previous interpretation, revised interpretation, reviewer and date. Preserve the original report. Do not backdate application receipts to make a missed entry look timely.
5. **Analyze comparable cohorts.** For a listing cohort, calculate confirmed collections / eligible listings after every listing's collection window closes. Deduplicate by donation ID. For time-to-claim, use request creation minus listing creation and report median and sample size. Request creation is recorded, approval time is currently unavailable. For emissions or waste attribution, disclose missing observations, seasonality, changes in donor supply, displacement of existing distribution and extra travel.
6. **Publish with permission.** Separate verified counts, partner observations and modeled estimates in the report. Give period, scope, data definitions, denominators, missing weights and exclusions. Share aggregate records or synthetic examples. Omit precise locations, photos with identifying details and small subgroup counts that identify partners. Have partners review the report and document corrections.

## Field report outline

Purpose and partner roles · period and baseline · eligibility and handling rules · metric definitions · funnel from listing through receipt · verified vs unweighed handoffs · missing records and refusals · fairness and travel burden · staff time and pickup costs · limitations · partner feedback · decision for the next iteration.

Avoid converting kilograms into meals without a transparent partner-specific definition and serving evidence. Avoided waste, people fed and health benefits each need different evidence. The monthly CLI is suitable for operations and supplies part of the field report.
